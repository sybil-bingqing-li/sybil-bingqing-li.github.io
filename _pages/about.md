I am a 2nd year PhD student in philosophy at UT Austin. I am primarily interested in philosophy of language, logic, and AI (large language models).

I earned my B.S. in Computer Science and Philosophy from Trinity College, Hartford, Connecticut.
Before starting my PhD in 2025, I spent 8 years working as a software engineer.

You can reach me at sybil.li@my.utexas.edu.
